# About Me
I build autonomous robotic and UAV systems with a focus on first-principles control, embedded architecture, and system-level reliability.

My work spans both ground and aerial platforms, where I design systems end-to-end — from low-level control to partially autonomous operation.

I engineered **PlanterBot**, a 7-actuator autonomous robot that secured a **Top 3 national finish at the ITU AI for Good Youth Challenge India 2025**. The system achieved ~98% operational reliability and ~0.76% positional drift over 2.6 m using encoder-based odometry and deterministic multi-actuator coordination.

In parallel, I’m developing the **SkySense UAV series**, transitioning from a custom Arduino-based flight controller to a Pixhawk + Raspberry Pi architecture integrating IMU, GPS, LiDAR, and vision for autonomous environmental sensing.

I also conduct experimental work in robot navigation, including an empirical study on encoder-based odometry across surface and speed conditions, identifying surface-dependent error behavior and a potential slip indicator (r = 0.805) in low-cost systems.

My approach emphasizes building systems that are predictable, measurable, and grounded in first principles — from control loops to full-stack autonomy.
